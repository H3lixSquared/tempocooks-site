Quick answers

Which recipes work? Paste a link from almost any recipe website, or import a screenshot. Tempo keeps the recipe's own words and just puts the steps in the fastest order.

Can two people cook together? Yes. Tap Invite Partner on the recipe and your partner types the code on their phone under Join a cook.

My timer didn't ring. Make sure notifications are on for Tempo in your iPhone's Settings. On iOS 26, allow Tempo's alarms so timers ring even when your phone is locked or on silent.

Does Tempo cost money? Tempo is free right now.
